# Engineering Albania — Leave & Time Off Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees in the Albania engineering office are entitled to **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and unused days may be carried over until **31 March** of the following year.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least **5 working days** in advance. Requests are approved by your engineering team lead and confirmed by HR. For absences longer than 10 consecutive days, approval from the office manager is required.

## Public holidays
The Albania office observes all official national public holidays. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is granted as a substitute day off.

## Sick leave
Sick leave must be reported to your team lead on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for absences of **3 or more consecutive days**.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is granted according to Albanian labour law. Paternity leave of **5 working days** is available within the first month after the birth of a child.
