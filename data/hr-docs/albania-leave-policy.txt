RAGnarok HR Demo — Albania Leave Policy

Annual Leave
Employees in the Albania office are entitled to annual leave according to local employment law and company policy.
To request annual leave, submit a request through the HR portal at least 14 days before the planned start date.
Your line manager must approve the request before leave is confirmed.

Sick Leave
If you are unable to work due to illness, notify your manager on the first day of absence.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Contact HR at hr-albania@company.com for questions about sick leave procedures.
