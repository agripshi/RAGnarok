# Engineering Albania — Working Hours & Remote Work

## Standard working hours
The standard working week in the Albania engineering office is **40 hours**, Monday to Friday. Core hours are **10:00–16:00**, during which all engineers are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration. Start and end times are flexible around the core hours.

## Remote and hybrid work
Engineers may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Remote work must be agreed with your team lead and recorded in the team calendar. At least **2 days per week** on-site are required to support pairing, onboarding and team rituals.

## Overtime
Overtime is voluntary and must be approved in advance by your team lead. Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu (TOIL) at an equivalent rate, to be used within the same quarter.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and necessary peripherals for both office and remote work. Hardware issues should be reported to IT support through the HR portal.
